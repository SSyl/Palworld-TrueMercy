[h1]True Mercy[/h1]
Stops burn and poison from killing a pal that Mercy is keeping alive for capture.

We've all been there... The Mercy Ring, Pal Tamer's Glasses, and the Mercy Hit passive stop you knocking a pal below 1 HP, but then a burn ticks and kills it at 1 HP anyway. Poison does the same. Your 10,000 damage fireball can't kill the pal, and then a 1 damage burn tick does.

With this mod, burn and poison can no longer take that last point of health while Mercy applies. They still hurt normally the whole way down though. Only the final point doesn't kill them. Capture rates and damage are untouched.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3625223587]UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)[/url] (Steam Workshop)
[/list]
Subscribe to both this and UE4SS Experimental, then enable them in-game under Options -> Mod Management and restart.

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
[list]
[*]Playing solo, or hosting for friends from inside the game: subscribe on your own PC.
[*]Dedicated server: install it on the server, not on your PC.
[*]Joining someone else's server: you don't need it. Only the machine running the world does.
[*]Subscribing on your PC while you play on someone else's server does nothing at all.
[/list]

[h2]What's protected[/h2]
A pal survives at 1 HP if it was burned or poisoned by:
[list]
[*]You, wearing the Mercy Ring or Pal Tamer's Glasses
[*]A pal you're riding, while you wear the ring or glasses
[*]Any pal with the Mercy Hit passive, when you're not riding it
[/list]

[h2]What isn't[/h2]
[list]
[*]Your pal fighting on foot, if the ring or glasses is your only source
[*]A pal you're riding that has Mercy Hit, if you aren't wearing the ring or glasses
[*]Lava, campfires and other environmental fire
[*]Wild pals fighting each other, unless one has Mercy Hit
[/list]
Riding a pal swaps whose Mercy counts, from the pal's to yours. That's the base game, not this mod.

[h2]Config Options[/h2]
Optional, in Mods\TrueMercy\Scripts\config.lua:
[list]
[*][b]ProtectFromBurn[/b] (default true): stops burn taking a protected pal's last point of health.
[*][b]ProtectFromPoison[/b] (default true): the same for poison.
[*][b]ProtectHumanNPCs[/b] (default true): human NPCs get the same protection as pals. Set it to false if you're running a mod like No Mercy For Humans.
[*][b]DebugLogging[/b] (default false): logs each pal the mod protects or skips, and why. Noisy in normal play.
[/list]
Anything missing or mistyped falls back to its default and says so in the log.

[h2]简体中文 (Simplified Chinese)[/h2]
[b]真・手下留情 (True Mercy)[/b]
完整说明请参阅上方英文部分。

当「手下留情」效果生效、目标帕鲁被保留血量用于捕获时，点燃与中毒伤害无法将其击杀。

相信大家都遇到过这种情况。慈悲戒指、帕鲁驯养师眼镜以及「手下留情」被动技能，能防止你直接把帕鲁血量打到 1 点以下，但后续的点燃伤害依旧会在帕鲁只剩 1 点血量时将其击杀。中毒也是同理。

你释放一万伤害的火球无法击杀帕鲁，结果点燃每次跳动的 1 点伤害却直接完成了击杀。

安装本模组后，「手下留情」效果生效期间，点燃与中毒无法夺走目标最后一点生命值。但在血量降到 1 点之前，点燃与中毒依旧正常造成伤害，只有最后一点血不会被夺走。捕获概率、伤害数值均不会被改动。

[b]前置需求[/b]
UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)（Steam 创意工坊）
需同时订阅本模组与 UE4SS Experimental，然后在游戏内「选项 → 管理Mod」中启用两者，并重启游戏。

[b]多人游戏[/b]
[list]
[*]单人游戏，或在游戏内联机邀请好友：在自己的电脑上订阅即可。
[*]专用服务器：安装在服务器上，而不是你自己的电脑。
[*]加入他人的服务器：你无需安装。只有运行世界的那台机器需要。
[*]在他人服务器上游玩时，自己订阅本模组不会产生任何效果。
[/list]

[b]受保护的情况[/b]
帕鲁在以下来源造成的点燃或中毒下会保留 1 点生命值：
[list]
[*]你本人，且佩戴慈悲戒指或帕鲁驯养师眼镜
[*]你骑乘的帕鲁，且你佩戴上述装备之一
[*]任何带有「手下留情」被动的帕鲁，且你未骑乘它
[/list]

[b]不受保护的情况[/b]
[list]
[*]你的帕鲁在地面作战，而「手下留情」来源仅为戒指或眼镜
[*]你骑乘的帕鲁带有「手下留情」，但你未佩戴戒指或眼镜
[*]熔岩、篝火等环境火焰
[*]野生帕鲁之间的战斗，除非其中一方带有「手下留情」
[/list]
骑乘帕鲁时，「手下留情」的判定来源会从帕鲁切换为你本人。这是游戏原本的机制，并非本模组所致。

[b]配置选项[/b]
可选，位于 Mods/TrueMercy/Scripts/config.lua：
[list]
[*][b]ProtectFromBurn[/b]（默认 true）：阻止点燃夺走受保护帕鲁的最后 1 点生命值。
[*][b]ProtectFromPoison[/b]（默认 true）：中毒同理。
[*][b]ProtectHumanNPCs[/b]（默认 true）：人类 NPC 获得与帕鲁相同的保护。若你在使用 No Mercy For Humans 之类的模组，请设为 false。
[*][b]DebugLogging[/b]（默认 false）：记录每一个被保护或被跳过的帕鲁及其原因。正常游玩时日志量较大。
[/list]
缺失或拼写错误的配置项会回退为默认值，并在日志中提示。
