[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Without this mod, burn and poison ignore Mercy. You weaken a pal down to 1 HP so you can throw a sphere, and then the fire finishes it off anyway.

This mod stops burn and poison taking that last point of health while Mercy applies. They still do full damage the whole way down to 1 HP. Nothing else changes.[/color][/size]

[size=4][b]What's protected[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]A pal survives at 1 HP if it was burned or poisoned by:[/color][/size]
[list]
[*][size=3][color=#D4D4D8]You, wearing the Mercy Ring or Pal Tamer's Glasses[/color][/size][/*]
[*][size=3][color=#D4D4D8]A pal you're riding, while you wear the ring or glasses[/color][/size][/*]
[*][size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Any pal with the Mercy Hit passive, when you're not riding it[/color][/size][/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]What isn't[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Your pal fighting on foot, if the ring or glasses is your only source[/color][/size][/*]
[*][size=3][color=#D4D4D8]A pal you're riding that has Mercy Hit, if you aren't wearing the ring or glasses[/color][/size][/*]
[*][size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Lava, campfires and other environmental fire[/color][/size][/*]
[*][size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Wild pals fighting each other, unless one has Mercy Hit[/color][/size][/*]
[/list]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Riding a pal swaps whose Mercy counts, from the pal's to yours. That's the base game, not this mod.[/color][/size]

[size=4][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Only one machine needs this, and it's whichever one is running the world:

  Playing solo - your own PC.
  Hosting for friends from inside the game - your own PC. Your friends install nothing.
  Dedicated server - the server. You and everyone joining install nothing.

Putting it on your own PC while you play on someone else's server does nothing at all.

1. Install UE4SS for Palworld on that machine.
2. Copy the TrueMercy folder into its UE4SS Mods folder, so you end up with:
  Mods\TrueMercy\Scripts\main.lua
  Mods\TrueMercy\Scripts\config.lua
  Mods\TrueMercy\enabled.txt
3. Start the game.[/color][/size]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Optional, in Mods\TrueMercy\Scripts\config.lua:

ProtectFromBurn (default true) - burn can't take a protected pal's last point of health.

ProtectFromPoison (default true) - the same for poison.

ProtectHumanNPCs (default true) - human NPCs get the same protection as pals. Set it to false if you're running a mod like No Mercy For Humans.

DebugLogging (default false) - logs each pal the mod protects or skips, and why. Noisy during normal play.

Anything missing or mistyped falls back to its default and says so in the log.[/color][/size]
